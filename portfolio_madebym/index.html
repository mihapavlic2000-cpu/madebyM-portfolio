Privacy Policy

1 — What data we collect

When you submit an inquiry through the contact form, we collect your name, email address, club or company name (if provided), and the content of your message. We collect no other personal data and use no tracking cookies.

2 — Why we process it

Your data is used solely to respond to your inquiry and, where relevant, prepare a quote. We do not send unsolicited mail and we do not use your data for advertising.

3 — Processing via Formspree

The form is technically processed by Formspree, Inc., which securely forwards your message to our email address over an encrypted connection (HTTPS/TLS).

4 — Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

5 — Retention and your rights

We keep your data only as long as needed to handle your inquiry or collaboration. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time — write to us at madebymp.studio@gmail.com.
